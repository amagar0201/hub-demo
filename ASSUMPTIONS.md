# Assumptions (rebuild of 2026-10-01)

The demo was rebuilt on the real app's current look and menus: Round 60 areas, look A, and the phone view with the Today screen.
Its made-up household was replaced with one of a deliberately different shape. Each item can be changed; say which number.

## Household (all invented)
1. **People:** Casey, Morgan and Ellis (16, in high school). No college student.
2. **Homes:** Willow, their own townhouse, with no home-equity line. Juniper is a rental duplex with units A and B and a line of credit on it. No sold homes.
3. **Car:** one, a 2021 Mazda CX-5 on a loan paid off in May 2028. No archived cars.
4. **Accounts and banks:** 11 accounts at 4 invented banks. No CDs, no foreign accounts, no gold.
5. **Today** is Thu Oct 1, 2026. Transactions run Aug 1 to Oct 1 from a fixed seed. Monthly history starts Jan 2025 and the plan runs to Dec 2027.
6. **Figures tie across pages.** Net worth = own − owe. Juniper's loan follows its own payment schedule. Rental Cash Flow, Net Worth and Mortgage show the same balance.

## Left out on purpose
7. **No immigration-investment tab, no Immigration tab, no FBAR or foreign-income cards.**
8. **No lock screens.** Nothing is saved: every edit, ✓, swipe and Undo only shows a note.

## Publishing
9. **Public repo on GitHub Pages.** You push each update yourself.
10. **Two private checks must pass before every commit.**
    - The leak check finds no listed term.
    - The shape check: the household's counts and totals must be far from the real ones.
